# Project F1: Moodle Activity

**Longitudinal Changes in Moodle Activity and Their Predictive Power for Student Results**

## Team

- Marina Gorelikova
- Denis Artemjev
- Ander Hinn

Topic proposed by Reimo Palm (University of Tartu).

## Description

We analyse coded daily Moodle activity logs of students and teachers from the Theoretical Computer Science course (University of Tartu, 2019–2025). Learning and teaching have been disrupted by COVID-19 and the rise of large language models, and we want to see how activity patterns changed and whether they predict final results.

## Goals

1. Analyse how student and teacher activity patterns in Moodle changed over 2019–2025.
2. Build models that predict students' final results from their Moodle activity.
3. Test whether models trained on earlier cohorts generalise to later cohorts.
